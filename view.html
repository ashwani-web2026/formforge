Points Rush → Champion Rush System

In the Grand Finals phase at Noida Indoor Stadium, teams first compete in the **Points Rush** stage. Performance here grants tier-based head-start points. In the **Champion Rush** stage, every elimination point and Booyah counts on top of head-start points to decide the FFMIC 2026 Champion!
